OpenAI tiktoken encoding data
================================

This application bundles the following tokenizer vocabulary files for offline
prompt-size calculation:

- cl100k_base.tiktoken
  Source: https://openaipublic.blob.core.windows.net/encodings/cl100k_base.tiktoken
  SHA-256: 223921b76ee99bde995b7ff738513eef100fb51d18c93597a113bcffe865b2a7
- o200k_base.tiktoken
  Source: https://openaipublic.blob.core.windows.net/encodings/o200k_base.tiktoken
  SHA-256: 446a9538cb6c348e3516120d7c08b09f57c36495e2acfffe59a5bf8b0cfb1a2d
